Rules

  1. Each player (or team) has 4 weights. Players alternate shots until all 8 have been played. That is one round.
  2. A weight must fully clear the foul line to stay in play. Anything short is removed.
  3. Weights that fall off the table or into the gutter are out for that round.
  4. Only one side scores each round: the side whose weight is closest to the far end.
  5. That side scores every one of its weights that sits farther than the opponent’s farthest weight.
  6. Scoring zones (weight fully past the line, viewed from above):
    • Zone 1: 1 point
    • Zone 2: 2 points
    • Zone 3: 3 points
    • Zone 4: 4 points
  7. A weight touching a zone line counts as the lower zone.
  8. A weight hanging over the far end scores 5 points (Hanger).
  9. After the round is scored, play the next round from the other end.
  10. First to the agreed total (usually 15 or 21) wins.
  11. The hammer is the last weight of the round. Either the round winner keeps it, or the teams take turns.
